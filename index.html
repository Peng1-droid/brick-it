Keep the front of your artwork and give it depth. Transparent cutouts work best. For a solid background, removal uses the top-left colour and only removes matching areas connected to the image edge.

Or start with a 3D model

Import a self-contained GLB, OBJ geometry or STL (up to 25 MB / 100,000 triangles). GLB keeps embedded colours and textures; OBJ/STL may use a single colour. Closed meshes work best. Imported models use voxel detail and orientation, not bust depth or background removal.

      How ordering works

      LEGO Pick a Brick is LEGO’s own shop for single pieces. “Buy at LEGO” downloads your list (). On Pick a Brick, click “Upload List”, choose the file, then check your bag: LEGO lists any piece it couldn’t add. Uploading a list isn’t available in every country: check Pick a Brick for yours.

      BrickLink is a marketplace of independent shops selling single pieces, including the ones LEGO doesn’t sell or has out of stock. “Buy on BrickLink” copies your list. Sign in (with a LEGO account), open Want → Upload, choose “Upload BrickLink XML format”, paste, then open the new wanted list and use “Buy All” to find shops.

      Questions

      Is my image uploaded anywhere?

      Upright busts, flat reliefs, model imports and exports run on your device. Only the optional OpenRouter generation button sends the selected artwork to your configured AI provider and uses your OpenRouter credits.

      Can I really build it?

      Every model is checked by computer: all pieces are standard parts in real colours, every piece is attached to the base (0 floating), no two pieces overlap (0 collisions) and the centre of mass sits over the base. The site shows these checks for your model and says so plainly if one fails. Models have not been built with real bricks yet, so treat your first build as a test. A few colours can be hard to find in large plates.

      How do I get the bricks?

      Under “Order the bricks”: download the Pick a Brick file and upload it on LEGO Pick a Brick (“Upload List”), and/or download the BrickLink list and upload it on BrickLink (Want → Upload). The site tells you how many lots LEGO sells, and “Prefer parts available at LEGO” rebuilds your model with those parts only. You order and pay on those sites, never here.

      What are the clear pieces?

      Some Punks have details that touch nothing, like cigarette smoke. They are held up by small clear bricks, and the site tells you when it adds them.

      Is this official?

      No. Brick It is a free, open-source, unofficial fan project, not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by the LEGO Group, BrickLink or the CryptoPunks project. It sells nothing and earns nothing: no affiliate links, no commission, no tracking.
